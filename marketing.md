## Marketing Psychology Tactics

### 1. **Social Proof**
- Add testimonials from students/universities
- Show usage statistics ("500+ students already managing their semester")
- Logos of partner institutions

### 2. **Scarcity & Urgency**
- "Limited spots available for Fall semester"
- Countdown timer for enrollment deadline
- "First 100 users get premium features free"

### 3. **Loss Aversion**
- "Don't lose track of deadlines again"
- "Stop losing money on missed payments"
- Frame as preventing negative outcomes

### 4. **Anchoring**
- Show premium price first, then discounted price
- "Originally $99/mo → Now $29/mo for students"

### 5. **Reciprocity**
- Free trial or freemium tier
- Downloadable academic planner as lead magnet
- Free study tips newsletter

### 6. **Commitment & Consistency**
- Start with small ask (sign up for newsletter)
- Then upgrade to free trial
- Then convert to paid

### 7. **Authority**
- "Designed by educators, built for students"
- Expert endorsements
- University partnerships

### 8. **Bandwagon Effect**
- "Join 10,000+ students"
- "Most popular plan" badge
- Trending indicators

## Conversion Optimization

### Hero CTA Button
- **Primary**: "Get Started Free" (green #60FC40)
- **Secondary**: "Watch Demo" (outlined)

### Trust Signals Below CTA
- No credit card required
- Free for students
- Cancel anytime

### Exit Intent Popup
- Offer downloadable academic planner
- Capture email for newsletter

### A/B Test Ideas
1. Button text: "Get Started" vs "Start Free" vs "Try Free"
2. Hero headline: Benefit-focused vs Problem-focused
3. Social proof: Testimonials vs Statistics vs Logos
